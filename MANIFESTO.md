I once needed a place to store my code. Not massive folders, not sprawling projects, just small snippets I could copy and paste when I needed them. I searched everywhere, endlessly, and found nothing that satisfied me. Ugly interfaces, programs that wouldn’t even open, websites built for corporations rather than real users.

Fed up, I decided to build my own. A place made for coders like me. Simple. Elegant. Reliable.

This site was born from that need.

If you want a space to store your code, with easy access, cloud synchronization, a clean and beautiful interface, and even GitHub Gist integration, this is your place too.
